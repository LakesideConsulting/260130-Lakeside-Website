Most strategy failures don't look like failures at first

They look like decisions that seem rational in isolation, but compound into expensive outcomes once capital is committed.

  • Capital deployed a year too early — or two years too late
  • Acquisitions that made sense on paper but don't deliver expected results
  • Technologies that work in trials, but never scale commercially
  • Markets entered without regulatory or supply-chain reality baked in
